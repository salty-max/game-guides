# App icon artwork

The three original illustrations were made with the built-in image generator. The square masters live beside each guide's export script, `scripts/icon-master.webp`. In each final master the square around the disk is the guide's dark `--surface`. [house-style.md](house-style.md#icon) has what core makes of them.

## Shared prompt

Create a single polished home-screen app icon tile for the Hundo guide series, exactly square and full bleed. Original iconic hand-painted game item illustration with simplified dimensional planes, confident silhouette and restrained texture. One unmistakable subject in the central disk, all important forms contained within a circle with diameter 72% of the square. Aim for about 60–65% canvas width for the subject. Spare background with a subtle central light gradient, darker towards edges. Matching family presentation across guides. Icon must work at 32 pixels. Flat square canvas edge to edge with no premade rounded corners, frame or border. No typography, letters, logo, app mockup, presentation background, numbers, watermarks, faces or small foreground decorations.

## Koholint

[Master](../apps/koholint/scripts/icon-master.webp) · [Home-screen icon](../apps/koholint/public/icons/icon-512.png)

Egg from The Legend of Zelda: Link’s Awakening. One large upright ivory egg with unmistakable broad raspberry-pink spots, nested on a simple low dark-jade stone crest with a subtle swath of emerald grass. Readable oval outline, warm eggshell highlight and big pink markings. Dark forest and teal-green background. Give it the playful rounded proportions of the Switch game while keeping the finish sophisticated.

## Echoes of Wisdom

[Master](../apps/echoes/scripts/icon-master.webp) · [Home-screen icon](../apps/echoes/public/icons/icon-512.png)

The Tri Rod from The Legend of Zelda: Echoes of Wisdom. A single clean golden magical wand, on a strong lower-left to upper-right diagonal, with chunky open triangular gold head and obvious short slender ornate handle. Bright honey-gold edges against deep royal violet and plum. Make the negative triangular opening and handle large enough to read on a home screen. A suggestion of magical violet light around the staff only.

## Red Dead Redemption

[Master](../apps/rdr/scripts/icon-master.webp) · [Home-screen icon](../apps/rdr/public/icons/icon-512.png)

Red Dead Redemption inspired. A single weathered Western gunslinger’s black cattleman hat with a creased crown, warm edge highlights, strong broad partly curled brim and a worn dark leather band. The terracotta disk takes its color from the guide’s light-theme primary. Dark tobacco brown and warm peach highlights echo the dark-theme surface and primary. Frontier print color and real leather material. Center the hat seen only slightly from above. Keep the brim compact enough to survive a circular crop.

## Tab drawings

A painting shrunk to 16px loses its subject, so each guide also has a hand-drawn tab icon, `scripts/favicon.svg`: a 32-unit tile with 7-unit corners in the guide's light `--primary`, with a flat version of the painting's subject.

- **Koholint**: a cream egg (the light `--paper`) with three raspberry spots.
- **Echoes of Wisdom**: the Tri Rod as one honey-gold stroke, triangle head and handle.
- **Red Dead Redemption**: the hat in peach (the dark-theme `--primary`) with a brown band.
