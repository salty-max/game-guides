The bar reads the game's points over 112, rounded down once (Mask Shards in fours and Vessel Fragments in threes first, then the scale), so it reads 99 with one shard left and 100 only with everything. `guide.test.ts` pins both. ## Missables

The nine in `missables.ts` each rest on two hosts that state the same condition: the wiki and the Steam guide or fextralife. slashkeys.com was dropped as a second host: it says it checks its claims against the wiki and credits the wiki for some, so like teemo it isn't independent of it. None of them costs a percent: the Troupe's finale counts the same through Nightmare King Grimm or Banishment ([wiki Completion](https://hollowknight.wiki/w/Completion_(Hollow_Knight)) and the game's code). That nothing the game counts can be missed at all is what the audit's research found, not something two hosts say outright, so the guide doesn't claim it.

On one host only, so kept off the strip:

- Quirrel's other meetings close on their own triggers: the Lake of Unn on the Mantis Claw, the Black Egg Temple on the room west of the False Knight, the City on Soul Master, and all of them on the Teacher's Archives ([wiki Quirrel](https://hollowknight.wiki/w/Quirrel)). The route meets each before its trigger.
- Cloth leaves the Fungal Wastes once you have the Mantis Claw ([wiki Cloth](https://hollowknight.wiki/w/Cloth)). The route meets her before the Claw, so a wrong wiki costs nothing.
- Zote also dies in his trap if you enter either City room between Relic Seeker Lemm and Cornifer, or Deepnest's Hot Spring room, before freeing him ([wiki Zote](https://hollowknight.wiki/w/Zote_the_Mighty)). The route frees him first.
- A charm Divine is holding earns no percent until you take it back ([wiki Completion](https://hollowknight.wiki/w/Completion_(Hollow_Knight))).

Checked and not missable: Nightmare King Grimm or Banishment (both count), the four Simple Keys (four locks), the Grey Mourner’s flower (a broken one can be fetched again), Quirrel at the Blue Lake, the Crossroads' infection (no item is lost), Sly's stock, Essence, and Cornifer's maps (Iselda sells them). A Fragile charm broken by death still counts (the game's code).

## Route decisions

The audit found rows that sat before what they need, so Next up offered them while they were impossible. Each moved; its id didn't.

- `quest-lake-quirrel` comes after Hornet: the Lake of Unn opens only once she's beaten ([wiki Greenpath](https://hollowknight.wiki/w/Greenpath), Steam guide ch 3).
- `quest-cloth-fungal` comes before the Mantis Claw (see Missables).
- Salubra's notches need that many charms owned: 18 are first possible in chapter 4 and 25 in chapter 6 (the charm rows counted through the route). `notch-salubra-eighteen` is in chapter 4 and `notch-salubra-twenty-five` in chapter 9, on the Crossroads return.
- `pale-ore-seer-ore` needs 300 Essence, about 100 by chapter 4. It's the first of the Seer's rewards in chapter 10 ([wiki Seer](https://hollowknight.wiki/w/Seer)).
- `pickup-lantern` (1,800 Geo) is the first row of chapter 6, before Deepnest's dark rooms; the draft had it in chapter 1, where few readers have that much.
- `quest-quirrel-deepnest` comes after the Tram Pass: he's at the Hot Spring only once the Failed Tramway has been visited ([wiki Quirrel](https://hollowknight.wiki/w/Quirrel), Steam guide ch 8).
- `route-dirtmouth-lift`, `grub-peak-mimic` and `grub-peak-upper` need Monarch Wings: they're a chapter 5 group after the Wings, where the Steam guide's ch 12 takes them.
- `charm-sharp-shadow` is behind a Shade Gate ([wiki Sharp Shadow](https://hollowknight.wiki/w/Sharp_Shadow)), so it comes in chapter 8, after the Shade Cloak.
