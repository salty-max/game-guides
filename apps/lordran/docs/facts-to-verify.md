## Achievements: a source, never shown

Dark Souls Remastered keeps no achievement list of its own. Its 41 achievements are the platforms' (Steam, Xbox, PlayStation trophies), and the Switch version has none (one host, below), so since 19 November 2026 the guide shows none: no target, no category, no chip and no achievement named in a row (the rule is core's, `data.test.ts`, and `guide.test.ts` pins it here, Knight’s Honor included). Steam’s list stays a source, the way the counts above use it to check nothing is missing.

- **No achievements on Switch.** One host says it outright: [gamepressure’s trophy list](https://www.gamepressure.com/darksouls/trophies/zfaf3e) (read directly) says they can be earned on every platform but the Switch, which has no system for them. The user said the same when making the rule (19 November 2026). No page found names an in-game list on any platform: fextralife’s trophy guide (read directly) and Steam’s list give the same 41 platform awards, and neither mentions the Switch. To settle it with a second host: a Switch review or Nintendo’s store page that says so.
- **What became of the 41 rows.** No id changed, and one row was dropped. The 29 that are also a boss, covenant, ring, maximum reinforcement or ending count once, as that (they used to count twice). The 7 route steps (the first bonfire, the class gear, Firelink, both bells, the Lordvessel and Anor Londo) are directions now and count nothing. Of the 5 that were only achievements, 4 (`achievement-knights-honor`, `-wisdom-of-a-sage`, `-bond-of-a-pyromancer`, `-prayer-of-a-maiden`) are the last route steps in chapter 13, worded as checks of the rare weapons and the three spell schools, which their own targets already count. The fifth, `achievement-the-dark-soul` (every other achievement), was dropped on the user's decision (19 November 2026): it was a platform award and nothing more, and a reader's old tick on it is ignored: saved checks for an unknown row are dropped on load, and a synced ledger keeps it without applying it. So the id is retired: a new row must never take it, or synced readers would find it already ticked.

## One-way choices

`data/choices.ts` holds six. The first cycle takes the way the guide recommends, and the other way is a row in chapter 13, for NG+ or NG++. Both count. Each choice's exclusion rests on two hosts (read 19 Nov 2026):

- **Frampt or Kaathe** (`quest-lordvessel-frampt`, then `cycle-ng-plus-plus-kaathe` in NG++). If Kaathe takes you to place the Lordvessel, Frampt leaves until the next playthrough ([wikidot Kaathe](https://darksouls.wikidot.com/darkstalker-kaathe), [fextralife Frampt](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Kingseeker+Frampt)); that Kaathe leaves when Frampt places it is wikidot's alone. Kaathe's way needs this cycle's Covenant of Artorias: Sif drops it each cycle ([fextralife](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Covenant+of+Artorias)). Kaathe's way is in NG++ because NG+ can't take it and keep the young Sif scene: Kaathe needs the Kings dead before the vessel is placed, the Kings need the Covenant of Artorias, which doesn't carry over ([wikidot](https://darksouls.wikidot.com/covenant-of-artorias), [fextralife](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Covenant+of+Artorias)) and comes from adult Sif, and the young Sif scene needs the Archives, which open only once the vessel is placed. The serpent doesn't decide the ending: [wikidot's Kiln page](https://darksouls.wikidot.com/kiln-of-the-first-flame) and [fextralife's Endings](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Endings) set both after Gwyn. **Not settled:** joining the Darkwraiths and keeping Frampt (`covenant-darkwraith`, `quest-lordvessel-frampt`) follows wikidot's Homeward route; [fextralife Darkwraith](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Darkwraith) says Frampt disappears once you join. No page says Darkwraith membership carries into NG+, which `cycle-ng-plus-sif` says.
- **Lautrec and Anastacia** (`quest-lautrec-invasion`, then `cycle-ng-plus-lautrec`). He kills her once the second bell is rung or the Parish and Blighttown Fire Keeper Souls are both taken, unless he's killed first at Firelink ([wikidot](https://darksouls.wikidot.com/knight-lautrec-of-carim), [fextralife](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Knight+Lautrec)). Killed early he drops the Ring of Favor and Protection and Humanity, not his armour (fextralife). That his Anor Londo invasion then never happens follows from it but neither host says it, so no row claims it. `quest-anastacia-return` follows the recommended branch.
- **Patches's apology** (`quest-patches-forgive`, then `cycle-ng-plus-patches`). Refusing gives Twin Humanities ([wikidot](https://darksouls.wikidot.com/patches), [fextralife](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Patches+the+Hyena)); that forgiving sets prism stones toward a hidden bonfire is wikidot's alone. Both put the cleric question before he kicks you into the pit, and `quest-patches-forgive` now says so. **Not verified:** whether he still moves to Firelink after a refusal; no host ties the move to the answer, and none says he moves after one. The first cycle forgives him, so `quest-patches` and the Crescent Axe don't rest on it.
- **Solaire and the red-eyed bug** (`quest-solaire-shortcut`, then `cycle-ng-plus-solaire`; it was a missable until it became a choice). Left alive when you enter Lost Izalith, the bug sends Solaire mad (wikidot adds the Chaos shortcut as a way in that does it too), and he attacks near the Bed of Chaos fog; his sign at Gwyn is gone ([wikidot](https://darksouls.wikidot.com/solaire-of-astora), [fextralife](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Solaire+of+Astora); the choice keeps the missable's [Gold Phantom](https://www.goldphantom.com/tools/ds1/npc-quest-tracker/) source). **Not verified:** what he drops when killed mad, and whether he drops the Sunlight Maggot; the row claims neither. `quest-solaire-saved` follows the recommended branch.
- **How Siegmeyer leaves the pit** (`quest-sieg-ash`, then `cycle-ng-plus-siegmeyer`). Above half health he goes to Ash Lake with Sieglinde (the slab), below it he dies and drops the Speckled Stoneplate Ring, and the ring and the slab exclude each other ([wikidot](https://darksouls.wikidot.com/siegmeyer-of-catarina), [fextralife](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Siegmeyer+of+Catarina)). **Disagree at exactly half:** wikidot says 50% or more goes to Ash Lake, fextralife more than 50% (`quest-sieg-ash` says "half health or more"). **Contradicted:** `ring-speckled-stoneplate` says killing the Chaos Eaters before he arrives ends his story with nothing; fextralife says it leaves you with the ring.
- **Ciaran and the Soul of Artorias** (`quest-ciaran`, then `cycle-ng-plus-dlc`). Handed over, the soul is hers for the Tracers ([wikidot](https://darksouls.wikidot.com/lord-s-blade-ciaran), [fextralife](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Lord's+Blade+Ciaran)); kept, it makes the Abyss Greatsword ([wikidot Soul of Artorias](https://darksouls.wikidot.com/soul-of-artorias), not on the choice's two pages). She asks again in NG+ whenever the soul is carried into the arena (wikidot). **Disagree:** one refusal and she's gone (wikidot), or two (Gold Phantom). Killing her keeps the soul and drops both Tracers, so the choice isn't strictly one-way; the guide doesn't route it.

Seen and kept as they were (they carry an earlier target, or only one answer has a row): the yes/no questions that close a shop (Laurentius's pyromancy question, `quest-laurentius`, `quest-laurentius-after-depths`, `extra-laurentius-goodbye`), Dusk's yes, `quest-reah` and `quest-reah-cleanup` (sources disagree on the Divine Blessing), `quest-logan-finish` (kill him only once he attacks), the Dark Lord and Fire endings (`ending`, counted on purpose), `ring-favor-protection` and `ring-speckled-stoneplate` (rings that exist only on one branch of Lautrec's and Siegmeyer's stories), `dlc-gear-gold-tracer` and `dlc-gear-dark-silver-tracer` (Ciaran's refusals), and `rare-weapon-priscilla-tail` (a strong blow kills her before the tail is cut).

## Route decisions

- NG joins the Darkwraiths without placing the Lordvessel for Kaathe: he carries you to the altar, you leave with a Homeward Bone, join him in the Abyss, then place the vessel with Frampt, who keeps his services ([wikidot Kaathe](https://darksouls.wikidot.com/darkstalker-kaathe)). The Dark Lord ending needs no covenant.
- NG kills Ornstein first, so Smough’s soul; NG+ kills Smough first, for Ornstein’s soul and the Leo Ring. Either order works across two cycles; Gold Phantom suggests the reverse.
- NG gives Artorias’s soul to Ciaran and uses Manus’s soul for Pursuers; NG+ forges the Abyss Greatsword and the Manus Catalyst.
- NG+ rescues young Sif before adult Sif. A Darkwraith cycle can’t: the Four Kings need adult Sif’s ring before the Lordvessel goes in, and the Broken Pendant is in the Archives behind it.
- Siegmeyer: the Ash Lake branch in NG, the Speckled Stoneplate Ring in NG+.
- NG’s Gwyn soul becomes Sunlight Spear in NG+; NG+’s becomes the Great Lord Greatsword in NG++, which takes the third Sif soul and the last forge, then Kaathe's way with the Lordvessel.
- Deadline-bound spells sit in their vendor’s chapter (Laurentius in 3, Siegmeyer’s Emit Force in 5, Logan in 7, Reah and the Gravelord dances in 8, Quelana in 9). Chapter 11 keeps the spells nobody can lose.

## Source conflicts and open questions

- Chaos Servant offerings: wikidot (covenant page, "Humanity (stat)") and the Cynic Steam guide say you offer soft Humanity; fextralife’s Chaos Servant page says Humanity items. Data follows wikidot: use the items, offer the soft Humanity.
- Lautrec's trigger for killing Anastacia: wikidot says both bells, or both the Parish and Blighttown Fire Keeper Souls, whichever first; fandom names the Blighttown soul alone. Data follows wikidot.
- Siegmeyer at the Izalith pit: wikidot gives three outcomes (all four Chaos Eaters dead before he leaps: ring, story ends; he survives under 50%: ring and 3 Humanity, story ends; 50% or more: story continues to Ash Lake) and warns that killing all four before he arrives ends his story with nothing.
- Laurentius at Firelink: wikidot gives no timing; the Cynic Steam guide has him reach Firelink only after Quelaag. The chapter 2 follow-up row says "next time you're at Firelink" rather than naming a shortcut.
- Reah after buying her miracles: fextralife and Steam guide 1393232553 say buying all her miracles sends her to the Archives, with no Divine Blessing exception; wikidot both says that and says leaving the Blessing unbought keeps her at the altar. The rows say the sources disagree. Her fate isn't counted; her three unique miracles are bought either way.
- Who forges the Manus Catalyst and the Great Lord Greatsword: wikidot's soul pages and boss-soul-items give the recipes but not the smith; the Giant Blacksmith page says only that he makes boss-soul weapons. The rows say the Giant Blacksmith.
- Shiva after absolution: wikidot says he sells again but stays in Darkroot Garden and never returns to Blighttown, and that kicking the bodyguard off the cliff blocks it; fextralife says you can absolve, rejoin and still buy weapons, and doesn’t mention Shiva turning hostile. The rows follow wikidot.

## By chapter

### Chapter 1

- **Solaire's spot in the Burg (`quest-solaire-bridge`).** The guide puts him down the steps on the right before you cross the Hellkite bridge, at a lookout. wikidot's Undead Burg page (read directly) says the same: at the foot of the stairs, look right and go down to the lookout. The Steam achievement guide 2673148886 (read directly) agrees: before crossing, head right down some stairs. wikidot's Solaire page (read directly) puts him "at the opposite end of the bridge" from the Hellkite instead. The row follows the two walkthroughs. A screenshot or map pin of Solaire's first spot would settle it.
- **Lautrec's Bell Gargoyles sign (`quest-lautrec-before-gargoyles`).** The guide says it's on a ledge behind the first ladder on the climb to the fog. wikidot's Bell Gargoyles page (read directly) says exactly that. wikidot's Lautrec page (read directly) only says "near each boss' fog door". The row follows the Bell Gargoyles page since it's more exact. One look in the game would confirm it.
- **The Dried Finger in the male merchant's stock (`pickup-residence-key`).** Settled, but the merchant's own wikidot page leaves it out. The guide says he sells it in Remastered. wikidot's Dried Finger page (read directly) and fextralife's Dried Finger page (read directly) both say Remastered moved it to the Undead Burg merchant. wikidot's Undead Merchant (Male) stock table (read directly) doesn't list it. - **Which end of the bridge the Basement Key door is at** (`pickup-basement-key` in chapter 1, `route-lower-shortcuts`).
